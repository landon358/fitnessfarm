Vitality 55+

Strength, balance and mobility that keeps you doing what you already love: on the water, in the garden, up and down the dock, on the floor with the grandkids. Low impact, sensibly loaded, coached the whole way.

If you have not trained in twenty years, this is the door. Nobody starts fit.

Kids classes

Movement, games and confidence. Not a shrunken adult workout. Kids learn to jump, hang, carry, squat and lose to each other with good grace, which is most of what strength training is anyway.

Parents can train in the class next door. Strollers park in the corner. That happens here more than you would think.
