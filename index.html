The Fitness Farm difference

Don't let the pole barn fool you

Rogue rigs. Concept2 rowers and bikes. Full barbell and bumper sets. A diamond plate floor that gets swept after every class. In summer the door rolls up and the field becomes part of the workout. It is not a commercial gym, and that is the entire point.
